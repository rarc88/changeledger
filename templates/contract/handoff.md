# Handoff Triage

## Triage friction at handoff; retrospect after completion

Before handing completed or stopped work to the human, classify friction and
unfinished observations instead of silently mixing them into the active change:

- Necessary to the active change's purpose: update its Specification, Plan and
  Log; it is not a new concern.
- An operational step (verify, commit, graduate, archive, close): execute or
  record it in the current flow.
- Independent, too large or materially broader: keep it outside the change and
  propose type, title and reason to the human, batching related proposals.
  Create the draft only after explicit authorization.
- Too vague for a backlog entry: mention it in the handoff, create no file.

Never mix independent concerns into active work or hold up an otherwise-ready
delivery. `discarded` records a decision, not a completed implementation cycle.
